# Prove the model and its presentation

Use this for emergent behavior, procedural content and rendering that promises to
represent a physical or systemic state. Do not impose scientific fidelity on an
arcade effect or stylized world that does not claim it.

## Separate the three verdicts

Record whether the component behaves correctly, whether the required visible behavior
exists, and whether it supports the intended play. A stable solver, conserved resource
or correct benchmark may pass while the generated shape or game remains inadequate.
Do not let improvement in a supporting metric overwrite the failed central verdict.

Keep the physical or logical state distinct from guidance fields, diagnostic overlays
and decorative effects. When the promise requires causality, the renderer must depict
the state that participates in interaction. A visible connection that carries nothing,
or an apparently destroyed obstacle that still blocks movement, fails that promise.
Cosmetic enhancement is valid when it does not misrepresent those affordances.

## Make the metric reject the actual defect

Measure the feature that distinguishes a good result from a plausible bad one. Whole
image overlap can be dominated by empty space or a solid center while missing a broken
branch network. A mean can conceal absent variation, saturation or local discontinuity.
Use a relevant region, distribution, contrast or topology check, with an inadequate
counterexample. Define a minimum amount of observable signal so an empty or uniform
output cannot earn a vacuous pass.

Where a claimed symmetry matters, use paired rotation or translation probes; where
discretization matters, vary spatial resolution or timestep. Compare like conditions
and apply the inverse transform when appropriate. Test multiple thresholds if a verdict
depends on segmentation. Do not demand exact equality from intentionally stochastic
models unless that is their contract. Do not assume that more resolution or noise fixes
a representation that cannot express the required behavior.

## Intervene on the cause

Remove or disable a proposed dependency and require its predicted consequence to
disappear; restore it and require the consequence to return. For example, interrupting
an actual transport path should stop transfer, including with stale cached flow data.
An observation before a local signal can arrive should not reveal distant information
unless the design grants that knowledge. These tests probe causality rather than event
counts. Give each added mechanism an observable responsibility before accumulating more.

## Distinguish references from recorded baselines

Label an artifact as a desired reference, an accepted regression target, or an observed
before-state. A reproducible failing screenshot remains a failure. When the user has
designated a fixed reference, preserve its bytes and framing or obtain authorization
to change it; do not quietly rebaseline to pass a check.

Compare the actual delivered renderer on the same relevant state, camera, exposure,
viewport and quality tier. Inspect normal play as well as any necessary close detail.
Capture metadata and integrity checks establish comparability, not visual acceptance.
An absent mode or tier is missing evidence, not a fixture to fabricate for the report.
